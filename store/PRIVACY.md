# Fredver privacy policy

_Last updated: November 6, 2026_

Fredver is an internal tool built by Columbia Basin Insurance ("we") for its own employees. It is not offered to the public.

## Who can use it
Only Columbia Basin Insurance employees with a @columbiabasininsurance.com Google Workspace account that an administrator has added.

## What Fredver accesses
- **Your Google account identity** (name and email address), to confirm you are an authorized employee.
- **Emails in the company's shared mailbox** (info@columbiabasininsurance.com), including attachments, so the team can read, assign, and answer them.
- **Your Gmail signature**, to add it to replies you send through Fredver.
- When you choose to send "from my address," Fredver sends that one message from your own mailbox, with a copy to the shared mailbox.

## What we store
On Columbia Basin Insurance's own Google Cloud project we store: conversation subjects, senders, and short previews of the latest message; assignments, statuses, internal notes, and an activity history; and the list of authorized employees. Full message bodies and attachments are read from Gmail when you open them and are not stored by Fredver.

In your browser, Fredver keeps your sign-in token in session storage (cleared when Chrome closes) and a few display preferences.

## Sharing
We do not sell data or share it with third parties. Data is processed only by Google services that host Fredver (Google Cloud and Gmail) under our Google Workspace and Google Cloud agreements, and only to provide Fredver's function.

## Retention and deletion
Records are kept while the shared mailbox is in use. An administrator can remove an employee's access or delete records on request.
